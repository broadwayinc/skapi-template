Skapi HTML Template

This is a plain HTML template that shows what you can build with Skapi, and how. Each page below demonstrates one feature with a short description and a working example. Open a page, try it, then view the page source to see how it is done.

Database, Realtime Chat, RTC Video Chat and Shared Drawing need a logged in user. They take you to the login page and bring you back afterwards.

  • User Account

    Sign up, log in, continue as a guest, verify an email address, reset or change a password, update a profile, remove and recover an account.

  • Newsletter

    Let visitors subscribe to your newsletter with their email address.

  • Send Inquiry

    A contact form that sends a message to you, the project owner.

  • Database

    A photo gallery: upload posts with files, like, comment, subscribe to other users and query posts in different ways.

  • Realtime Chat

    Group chat and private messages over a WebSocket connection.

  • RTC Video Chat

    Video calls, screen sharing, text chat and file transfer between two users with WebRTC.

  • Shared Drawing

    Two users draw on one canvas; every stroke travels over a WebRTC data channel.
