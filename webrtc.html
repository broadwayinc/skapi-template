RTC Video Chat

A video call between two users, straight from one browser to the other. skapi.connectRTC() calls the other user with the camera and the microphone, the callee gets an rtc:incoming message and answers with connectRTC() on it, and Skapi only carries the signaling. The screen, text messages and files travel over the same connection.

Allow the camera and the microphone when the browser asks. WebRTC works over HTTPS or on localhost. Open this page in another browser or device with a second account: both users appear in the list below. Click a video during the call to go fullscreen; Escape or the Back button leaves it.
